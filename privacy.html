Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Landmark-Video ("the Service") handles when a TikTok account is connected, and how that information is used and protected.

1. Information we handle

  • TikTok authorization data: when you authorize the Service through TikTok Login Kit, we receive access and refresh tokens that let the Service post videos to your account on your behalf.
  • Basic profile information: such as your TikTok display name or open ID, used only to identify the connected account (scope user.info.basic).
  • Videos and records: the videos the Service creates, and simple logs of what was posted and when.

We do not collect your TikTok password, direct messages, contacts or private videos.

2. How we use information

  • To upload and publish videos to the connected TikTok account.
  • To keep the connection working (for example, refreshing expired access tokens).
  • To keep basic records of posted content for troubleshooting.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. Data is only shared with service providers needed to run the Service, such as cloud storage and workflow automation tools, and with TikTok itself when posting content. These providers handle data only to deliver their services.

4. Storage and security

Tokens and credentials are stored in private, access-restricted systems and are never published publicly. We take reasonable measures to protect them, but no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Retention and deletion

Authorization data is kept only while the connection is active. When you revoke access or ask us to delete your data, tokens and related records are removed.

6. Your choices

  • Revoke access: in the TikTok app, go to Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services, and remove Landmark-Video. (Menu names may vary by TikTok version.)
  • Request deletion: email us at the address below and we will delete the data we hold about your connected account.

7. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
