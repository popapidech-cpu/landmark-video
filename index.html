Landmark-Video

Short travel videos about famous landmarks around the world.

What it does

Landmark-Video is a personal content tool that automatically creates short travel videos about well-known landmarks and places around the world, and publishes them to the owner's own TikTok account using the TikTok Content Posting API.

How it works

  • The owner connects their own TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit.
  • The tool generates a short video about a world landmark.
  • The video is posted to the connected TikTok account.
